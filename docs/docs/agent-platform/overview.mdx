# Build Your Agent Platform

Enterprises have moved past experimentation. AI agents are in production — handling customer support, automating workflows, generating code, and orchestrating complex business processes. But scaling from a handful of pilots to dozens (or hundreds) of agents introduces challenges that individual teams cannot solve alone.

---

## Why Agent Platforms Are Needed Now

The shift from "proof of concept" to "production fleet" exposes fundamental scaling challenges that require platform-level solutions.

## Platform Thinking for AI Agents

:::info Definition
**A platform is a shared enterprise foundation for building, deploying, discovering, governing, and operating AI Agents at scale.**
:::

Platform thinking applies the same principles that transformed application development (Kubernetes, service meshes, developer portals) to the world of AI agents. Instead of each team solving infrastructure, security, and observability independently, a platform team provides curated, self-service capabilities that accelerate delivery while maintaining governance.

| Without a Platform | With a Platform |
|---|---|
| Each team builds auth, memory, observability | Shared services, self-service onboarding |
| No visibility into agent fleet | Central registry and discovery |
| Inconsistent security posture | Policy-as-code, enforced guardrails |
| Cost surprises at month-end | Real-time attribution and budgets |
| Manual compliance reviews | Automated governance workflows |
| Siloed knowledge | Reusable patterns and templates |
