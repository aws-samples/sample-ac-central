## Customer Story

**Customer:** Parrot Analytics - Global content analytics company

**Challenge:** Media executives needing real-time audience and content intelligence but lacking technical skills to query complex datasets

**Solution:** Conversational agents providing content intelligence insights, enabling media executives to query audience data through natural language. The agent translates business questions into analytical queries and presents results with context.
