## Customer Story

**Customer:** Warner Bros. Discovery (WBD) - Global media and entertainment company

**Challenge:** Advertising RFP responses requiring manual data gathering across multiple systems, consuming significant sales team time

**Solution:** Agents automatically processing advertising RFP requests, gathering relevant data from multiple sources, and generating tailored responses for the media sales team. Reduces response time from days to hours while maintaining quality.
