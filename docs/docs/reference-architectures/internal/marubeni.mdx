## Customer Story

**Customer:** Marubeni Corporation — Major Japanese integrated trading and investment company

**Challenge:** Democratizing AI capabilities across a diverse conglomerate with varied technical maturity across business units

**Solution:** Enterprise platform democratizing AI agent access across the organization, with standardized templates and governance. Non-technical teams can deploy agents using pre-built patterns while central platform team maintains quality and compliance.
